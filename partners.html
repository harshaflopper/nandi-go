District Partner

Represent Nandi Go across your whole district. Promote approved trips through WhatsApp, Instagram, friends and local shops, and send bookings through the official Google Form or WhatsApp number.

Commission

  • ₹300–500 per confirmed person booking (final amount fixed in your agreement)
  • ₹1,000 extra for a group of 10+ bookings together
  • ₹5,000 bonus on 30 confirmed bookings in a month
  • Paid every Monday for the previous week's confirmed bookings

Your commitments

  • All customer advances are paid directly to Nandi Go's official UPI / bank account — partners never collect cash
  • Use only official posters, videos, logo and company-approved rates; no false promises
  • Do not work for another travel company for the same destinations (Chikkamagalur, Coorg, etc.)

How it works

From application to your first booking

  1. ApplyFill in the partner application form. It takes about 3 minutes.
  2. Screening callOur team contacts you on WhatsApp within 24 hours.
  3. VerificationShare your passport-size photo, full-length photo, a selfie for identity confirmation, KYC and bank account details. Bank details are locked once confirmed, so commission always goes to the verified account.
  4. Agreement & kitSign the District Partner Agreement and receive your Premium Business Kit and PVC ID card.
  5. One-day trainingLearn our packages, rates, booking process and official marketing material.
  6. Start earningPromote Nandi Go trips in your district. Commission is paid every Monday.

Verification documents are collected only during onboarding through the official channel — never through the public application form.
