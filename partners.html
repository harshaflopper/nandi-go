How it works

From application to your first booking

  1. ApplyFill in the partner application form. It takes about 3 minutes.
  2. Screening callOur team contacts you on WhatsApp within 24 hours.
  3. VerificationOnce approved, you get a partner code. Submit your photos, KYC and bank details in the KYC form on this page. Bank details are locked once submitted, so commission always goes to the verified account.
  4. Agreement & kitSign the Partner Agreement and receive your Premium Business Kit and PVC ID card.
  5. One-day trainingLearn our packages, rates, booking process and official marketing material.
  6. Start earningPromote Nandi Go trips in your district. Commission is paid every Monday.

Identity and bank documents are asked for only after approval, in the KYC form that needs your partner code — never in the public application. They are stored privately and seen only by the Nandi Go team.
