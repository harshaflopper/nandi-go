These terms apply to every trip booked on nandigo.co. The website and trips are run by L N P ENTERPRISES under the brand Nandi Go. By booking, you agree to these terms for yourself and for everyone in your booking.

1. Booking and payment

  • Choose a trip, travel style and batch date, and enter the name and age of every traveller.
  • Prices are in Indian Rupees and are per person, or per couple where shown. What is and is not included is listed on each trip page.
  • The full amount is paid online through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking). Your seats are confirmed only after the payment succeeds, and you then receive your e-ticket.
  • Please pay only on nandigo.co. Nandi Go never asks you to pay a partner or a personal bank account.

2. Traveller ID

  • Every traveller must provide a valid government photo ID before travel: masked Aadhaar, driving licence, voter ID or passport.
  • International trips need a valid passport (Nepal also accepts a voter ID for Indian citizens). Visas, if any, are the traveller's responsibility unless the trip page says they are included.
  • Travellers must carry the original ID on the trip. Hotels may refuse check-in without it, and no refund is due in that case.

3. Cancellation and refund policy

  • Cancellation by you: bookings are non-refundable. Seats, stays and transport are reserved for you as soon as you pay, so we cannot refund if you cancel, miss the pickup or leave the trip early.
  • Change of traveller: you may transfer your seat to another person at no charge if you tell us at least 48 hours before departure and share their ID.
  • Cancellation by Nandi Go: if we cancel a trip or batch for any reason, including too few travellers, you choose either a full refund or a free move to another date.
  • Weather, natural events and government orders: if a trip cannot run for reasons outside our control, we offer a new date. If no suitable date works for you, we refund the amount paid minus costs already paid to hotels and transport that we cannot recover, and we show you those costs.
  • How refunds are paid: to the same card, UPI or bank account you paid from, through Razorpay, within 7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Failed or double payments: if money is debited but the booking is not confirmed, it is refunded automatically, usually within 5 to 7 working days.
