Terms, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Please read carefully before booking. By paying the advance, you agree to all the terms below. These terms apply to every trip booked on nandigo.co, run by L N P ENTERPRISES under the brand Nandi Go.

1. Booking and advance payment

  1. 1.1 A booking is confirmed only after the advance amount is paid.
  2. 1.2 The advance is ₹500 per person or 50% of the total trip cost, whichever is higher, unless mentioned otherwise.
  3. 1.2a For trips that include or need a flight (for example Lakshadweep, Manali + Shimla, Nepal and Thailand), the advance is ₹2,000 per person.
  4. 1.3 The advance is paid to block your seat, bus, hotel and food. It is not a refundable deposit.
  5. 1.4 The balance must be paid 3 days before the trip, or on the day of travel before boarding, as informed by the Nandi Go team. You can pay the balance online from My trips.
  6. 1.5 The website shows the full trip price, the advance you pay now and the balance due before you pay. You may also choose to pay the full amount at once.

3. No-show policy (most important)

  1. 3.1 If the customer does not come on the trip day for any reason (health, personal, family, work, traffic or any other reason), no refund will be given.
  2. 3.2 The advance will be treated as a loss, because Nandi Go would have already paid for the hotel, bus seat, food and entry tickets in advance on your behalf.
  3. 3.3 No transfer to another trip is allowed for no-show cases. It will be treated as cancelled.

4. Late arrival and bus departure

  1. 4.1 The reporting time will be shared in the trip group 1 day before the trip. The customer must reach 15 minutes before the reporting time.
  2. 4.2 The bus will wait for a maximum of 10 minutes after the departure time.
  3. 4.3 If the customer does not arrive within 10 minutes, the bus will leave. This will be treated as no show, no refund.
  4. 4.4 Nandi Go will not wait longer, because waiting causes trouble and delay for other customers who arrived on time. No complaint will be accepted in this regard.
  5. 4.5 If the customer misses the bus, the customer must reach the destination at their own cost. No refund for missed transport.

5. Non-transferable booking

  1. 5.1 A booking is strictly non-transferable and is valid only for the person whose name is registered at the time of booking.
  2. 5.2 The customer cannot cancel and send another person in their place. No replacement is allowed.
  3. 5.3 No name change, transfer or resale of a booking is allowed after confirmation.
  4. 5.4 If a different person comes with the booking details, entry will be denied and it will be treated as no show, no refund.
  5. 5.5 This policy is for safety, hotel records and legal compliance.

6. ID proof mandatory

  1. 6.1 Every traveller must upload one government ID while booking, and must carry the original Aadhaar card or government ID during the trip.
  2. 6.2 The name on the ID proof must match the name given during booking. A mismatch will lead to denial of boarding.
  3. 6.3 No ID = no boarding = no refund.
  4. 6.4 ID proof is mandatory for hotel check-in as per hotel and government rules. International trips (Nepal, Thailand): a valid passport is required for every traveller. Domestic flight trips (Manali + Shimla, Lakshadweep): passport or Aadhaar.

7. Hotel and food booking

  1. 7.1 Hotel rooms are booked in advance based on confirmed bookings. The hotel has its own no-refund policy.
  2. 7.2 If the customer cancels late or does not come, hotel charges will be deducted from the advance.
  3. 7.3 Room sharing will be as per Nandi Go policy: 2, 3 or 4 sharing. A single room request will be charged extra.
  4. 7.4 The food menu is fixed as per the trip plan. No refund for food not consumed.

8. Trip change by Nandi Go

  1. 8.1 If a trip is cancelled by Nandi Go due to weather, a government order or unavoidable reasons, the customer will get a 100% refund or the option to shift to the next trip.
  2. 8.2 Nandi Go can change the bus, hotel, route or timing for safety reasons. No extra refund will be given for such changes.

9. Behaviour and safety

  1. 9.1 Customers must follow the Team Leader's instructions.
  2. 9.2 Alcohol, smoking, drugs and misbehaviour with other customers or staff are strictly prohibited.
  3. 9.3 Nandi Go has the right to remove any customer from the trip without refund for misbehaviour.
  4. 9.4 Customers are responsible for their own valuables, mobile, camera and luggage.

10. Final agreement
