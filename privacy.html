Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how L N P ENTERPRISES, which runs the travel brand Nandi Go and the website nandigo.co ("we", "us"), handles your personal data. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, WhatsApp number, email, and the name and age of every traveller in your booking, plus the trip, travel style and dates you choose.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Traveller ID (KYC): one government ID per traveller (masked Aadhaar, driving licence, voter ID or passport), needed for hotel check-in and, for some trips, travel permits.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Razorpay. Your card, UPI and bank details go directly to Razorpay; we never see or store them. We only receive the payment reference and amount.
  • Reviews and photos you choose to post, and private messages you send through the Nandi Secret Box (name and number optional).
  • Business partner applications: details and KYC documents submitted by District and Taluk Partner applicants.
  • On your device: we keep a few small items in your browser's local storage, such as a partner referral code for 30 days and links to your recent tickets. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. Why we use it

  • To make and confirm your booking, send your e-ticket, and contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your trip.
  • To check in every traveller at hotels and homestays and, where needed, to arrange permits.
  • To take payment, issue refunds when they apply, and keep accounts as the law requires.
  • To show reviews you have agreed to publish, and to improve our trips using your feedback.
  • To review partner applications.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Hotels, homestays and transport operators for your trip: only the traveller names and, where they must see it, the ID needed for check-in.
  • Razorpay, to process payments.
  • Our technology providers, who store or process data for us under contract: Supabase (database and file storage, servers in Mumbai, India), Vercel (website hosting) and Google (sign-in and our email).
  • Government authorities, only when the law requires it.

4. How we protect traveller IDs

  • ID files are stored in private storage with no public links. They are never shown on the website.
  • Only authorised Nandi Go staff, signed in with Google, can open them, and each viewing link expires after 5 minutes.
  • For Aadhaar we ask only for the masked Aadhaar, which shows the last 4 digits. We do not collect full Aadhaar numbers.
  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. How long we keep it

  • Traveller ID files: deleted automatically 30 days after the trip ends. IDs uploaded for a booking that is never paid are deleted when that booking expires.
  • Booking and payment records: kept for up to 8 years, as required by Indian tax and accounting laws.
  • Reviews: kept while they are published; you can ask us to remove yours at any time.
  • Secret Box messages and partner applications: kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent (for example, to have a review or an ID removed). Email us at nandigo2026@gmail.com from the email or number you booked with. We reply within 30 days. Some records, such as payment records, must be kept by law even after you ask us to delete your account.

7. Grievance officer

If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, write to our grievance officer at nandigo2026@gmail.com with the subject "Data grievance". We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

Bookings must be made by an adult (18 or above). Details of children travelling with a family are provided by their parent or guardian, who gives consent on their behalf.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Big changes will be shown on the website.
